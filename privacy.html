Privacy Policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

CrestPrep is a free Digital SAT prep site made by Saransh Shekhar, a high school student, for the Congressional App Challenge. This page explains, in plain language, what information the site uses and why.

What we collect

  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address, and a Google account ID. Google shares these with us through Supabase, the service that runs our sign-in. We use your first name to greet you and your account ID to recognize you when you come back. We never see your Google password.
  • Your study progress and Crest Cache: the questions you answer, the tests you add in Crest Ingest (scores, misses by skill, and your error log), your goal score, and your test date. This is saved in your own browser on your device, not on our servers.
  • Score reports and error log PDFs: when you choose a PDF, it is read inside your browser to fill in the form. The file itself is never uploaded to CrestPrep. We keep the numbers and text it contains in your Crest Cache, in your browser.
  • AI tutor: when you ask the AI tutor something or press "Analyze this test with Gemini," your message and the study information it needs (the practice question, or your test's scores, misses, and error log) are sent to Google's Gemini service to write a reply. CrestPrep uses Gemini's free tier, and Google may use what is sent to improve its products, so please don't type personal information (like your full name, address, or school) into the tutor or your error log.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't show ads.
  • We don't use your information for anything except running CrestPrep.

Other services the site uses

CrestPrep relies on tools from other companies, which have their own privacy policies: Supabase (sign-in), Google (sign-in and the Gemini AI tutor), Vercel (hosting), Desmos (the graphing calculator), and Google Fonts (lettering).

Age

CrestPrep is made for high school students preparing for the SAT. It is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has signed in, contact us and we will delete the account.

Deleting your information

You can clear your progress anytime with "Start over as a new student" on the dashboard or by clearing your browser's data for this site. To delete your sign-in account, email us and we'll remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
